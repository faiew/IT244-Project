The Smart Clinic Database System contains the following relationships:

1. Books

Entities:
Patient - Appointment

Cardinality:
1 : M

Description:
A patient can book many appointments, but each appointment belongs to exactly one patient.

2. Conducts

Entities:
Staff - Appointment

Cardinality:
1 : M

Description:
A staff member can handle many appointments, while each appointment is assigned to one staff member.

3. Works In

Entities:
Department - Staff

Cardinality:
1 : M

Description:
One department can have many staff members, and each staff member belongs to one department.

4. Leads To

Entities:
Appointment - Treatment

Cardinality:
1 : M

Description:
One appointment may result in multiple treatments, while each treatment belongs to one appointment.

5. Generates

Entities:
Appointment - Payment

Cardinality:
1 : M

Description:
One appointment can generate multiple payments, and each payment is related to one appointment.

6. Includes

Entities:
Treatment - Prescription

Cardinality:
1 : M

Description:
One treatment may include multiple prescriptions, while each prescription belongs to one treatment.

7. Specifies

Entities:
Medicine - Prescription

Cardinality:
1 : M

Description:
A medicine can appear in many prescriptions, while each prescription specifies one medicine.
